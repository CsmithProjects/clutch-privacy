Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026 · Version 2026-08-02.2

Clutch AI ("Clutch," "we," "our," or the "App") is operated by Conor Smith. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the App, why it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

The short version: Clutch does not sell personal information. Your saved chat history is stored in encrypted, account-scoped storage on your device. Content you submit for an AI feature is sent through our backend to the provider needed to answer that request only after you affirmatively allow AI processing. The consent screen names Supabase, DeepSeek, OpenAI, and Google Places, explains what each receives, and identifies DeepSeek's potential processing and storage in China. Optional product analytics and crash diagnostics are off by default. You can decline AI sharing, change those choices, revoke AI permission, or delete your account from the App.

2. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate your account and keep account-scoped data separated.
  • Provide Wingman, screenshot analysis, Profile Audit, Bio Writer, Date Spotter, Date Debrief, and Panic features.
  • Verify subscription access, enforce usage limits, and detect duplicate trial use among active accounts.
  • Send notifications you have enabled. Notification permission can be changed in your device settings.
  • If you separately opt in, process pseudonymous product analytics through PostHog.
  • If you separately opt in, process sanitized crash diagnostics through Sentry.
  • Receive and investigate an AI response report you choose to submit. Reports go to Clutch through Supabase and are not forwarded to an AI provider.
  • Investigate errors or abuse and protect the service.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and protect users, the App, and our service providers.

We do not sell or rent personal information, use third-party advertising SDKs, or share your conversations with other Clutch users.

3. AI Requests and Screenshots

When you request an AI feature, Clutch sends the content needed for that request from your device to a Supabase Edge Function. The function routes text-generation requests to DeepSeek and image-extraction requests to OpenAI. Date Spotter may also send a location-biased search to Google Places.

  • Before the first AI request, the App identifies the categories of content that may be shared, names the third-party recipients, where DeepSeek may process and store content, why each recipient receives it, and known retention limits. The App asks you to explicitly allow this sharing. Entering an AI screen by itself sends no prompt, image, or location. You may decline and continue using non-AI account and privacy controls. You can revoke permission from Account > Privacy controls; no new AI request will be sent from the App unless you allow processing again.
  • Supabase: receives the authenticated account/session identifiers, request content, and basic request metadata needed to authorize and route the request. Supabase does not receive a cloud chat-history archive from Clutch.
  • DeepSeek (People's Republic of China): receives prompts, extracted screenshot text, profile answers, saved context, relevant venue details, and, if you selected the 16–17 age band, that declared band in a safety instruction for text generation and safety checks. This content may be processed and stored in China. DeepSeek's public Open Platform terms do not state a fixed API-content retention period or promise that downstream API content is excluded from model improvement. DeepSeek's current public privacy policy also says downstream developer-app end-user processing is not covered by that public policy and says its general services are not intended for sensitive data, children's data, or precise geolocation. Clutch therefore keeps DeepSeek processing disabled unless a retained written agreement and review expressly cover this App's downstream users, dating/profile content, 16–17 age band, retention and deletion, training, subprocessors, security, and international transfers.
  • OpenAI: receives selected screenshots or profile images, the extraction instructions needed for the feature, and, if you selected the 16–17 age band, that declared band in a safety instruction. Clutch sets API response storage to off. Standard OpenAI API abuse-monitoring logs may still contain request content and be kept for up to 30 days, unless law requires longer retention or the project's separately approved data controls provide a shorter period.
  • Google Places: receives a venue search and approximate or precise coordinates only when you request nearby Date Spotter results. DeepSeek receives the resulting relevant public venue details, not the device location coordinate supplied separately to Google Places.
  • Only submit screenshots or messages that you have the right to use. Consider redacting names and other details that are not needed for the request. Dating content can reveal sensitive information such as sexuality, health, religion, or precise routines; do not include it unless it is necessary and you have permission to share it.
  • Before a selected screenshot is uploaded, the App uses on-device text recognition to flag likely names, handles, contact details, and locations. You can cover any recognized line in a separate upload copy and must visually review the image because automated recognition can miss details. This privacy scan and the mask selection run on the device.
  • Before any selected image can be read for upload, the per-image privacy review requires you to confirm both that you have permission to share the content and that you reviewed or redacted unnecessary personal details. That per-upload attestation is bound to the exact reviewed copy in memory; it is not restored from saved drafts or sent to an AI provider.
  • Clutch compresses selected images for transmission, and temporary transmission copies made by the App are removed after processing. Clutch does not add a copy of the selected image or its local file reference to saved Wingman or Date Debrief history. Text extracted from the image may be saved on your device when you keep the conversation or confirm a debrief transcript. The original remains in your photo library unless you delete it there.
  • Do not treat an AI request as a confidential or privileged communication. Permission lasts no more than 12 months, is versioned to this disclosure, and must be requested again after a material change to recipients, data categories, purposes, locations, or retention terms.

5. Storage and Security

  • Saved conversations and related private state are account-scoped and encrypted on your device. The encryption key is stored using the device's secure credential storage.
  • Authentication session material is kept in secure device storage. Operational account, subscription, usage, notification, consent, and AI response-report records are stored with Supabase.
  • Network requests use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Database access controls and server-side authorization are used to limit access to account data.
  • No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and store accounts secure, and contact us if you believe your Clutch account has been compromised.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • On-device private content: Saved conversations, bio versions, plans, and debriefs remain encrypted and account-scoped on your device until you delete the relevant record, choose “Sign out and remove data from this device,” delete your account, or remove the App or its data. Ordinary sign-out removes authentication but keeps this encrypted device copy for your next sign-in. Clutch limits each saved conversation to its most recent 1,000 messages.
  • AI request content: Clutch processes it for the request and does not add it to a cloud chat archive. Provider-side request and security records follow the provider-specific periods, contractual controls, and exceptions described in Section 3. Deleting your Clutch account does not by itself prove that a provider's security log was erased. Contact us for an applicable provider-side privacy request; we will use the available contractual or provider process and explain any legal or technical limitation.
  • AI response reports: The response identifier, task, app area, category, and optional displayed response content are stored in Supabase, linked to your account, while the account exists or until an applicable earlier deletion request is completed. Response content is excluded unless you separately turn on the off-by-default inclusion switch. Reports are not forwarded to DeepSeek, OpenAI, or another AI model provider and cascade when the account is deleted.
  • Active account records: Supabase account, cloud profile and preferences, entitlement, usage, consent, age band, referral, device-linking, notification, and moderation-report records are kept while the account exists or as otherwise explained here. In-App deletion deletes the authentication account and rows linked by account-deletion rules. A referral record may remain after a referrer deletes an account with its account link removed, while store/webhook security records and deleted data may remain temporarily in configured backups or security logs.
  • Rate-limit records: Account-linked PostgreSQL request buckets expire at the end of a window of no more than one hour and are deleted opportunistically on a later request. Daily Panic and Draft Review allowance rows older than seven days are also deleted opportunistically for that account. All remaining linked allowance rows cascade when the account is deleted. The App does not keep a separate email/device hash ledger after account deletion.
  • Optional analytics: New PostHog collection stops as soon as you turn analytics off. Existing records remain under the project's configured retention unless you request deletion. Account deletion queues deletion of the associated pseudonymous PostHog person and events.
  • Optional diagnostics: New Sentry collection stops as soon as you turn diagnostics off. Diagnostic events contain no Clutch account identifier and age out under the configured Sentry retention window; some event information may remain temporarily within shared error issues.
  • Account deletion: Before deleting the Supabase account, Clutch requests deletion of the associated RevenueCat customer and PostHog records and revokes Sign in with Apple authorization when Apple provides the required code. If Apple cannot provide a code, the App gives manual revocation instructions and asks you to confirm before continuing. The App then removes its private local data, notification state, telemetry choices, and device identifier.
  • Store and platform records: Deleting Clutch does not cancel a subscription or erase purchase, payment, or fraud-prevention records independently controlled by Apple or Google. Those records follow the store's policies and legal obligations.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can review or revoke third-party AI processing permission from Account > Privacy controls. AI features remain unavailable while permission is off, and non-AI account and privacy controls remain available.
  • You can report an AI response without including the response text. The separate response-content switch is off by default. Submitted reports are included in the in-App account export; contact us to request earlier deletion of a report.
  • Optional product analytics and optional crash diagnostics are separate, off by default, and can each be turned on or off at any time from Account > Privacy controls. Turning either off stops new collection immediately. Signing out also resets both choices on that device.
  • Location, photo-library, and notification access can be declined or changed in device settings. Some related features will not work without the relevant access.
  • You can delete individual conversations, saved bio versions, date plans, and date debriefs in the App. Cancelling an active date plan marks it cancelled and removes its reminders without falsely deleting the plan history.
  • You can delete your Clutch account from the account controls in the App. Apple-linked accounts may ask you to confirm with Apple so the authorization can be revoked. Cancel an active subscription separately through Apple App Store or Google Play so it does not renew.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of personal information. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity, and some records may be retained where permitted or required by law.

8. Age Requirement

Clutch is intended for users 16 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. Contact us if you believe an underage user has provided information to Clutch. The App stores only a declared 16–17 or 18+ band, not a birth date. Users in the 16–17 band receive additional non-explicit content and venue safeguards. DeepSeek processing remains disabled unless the written agreement described in Section 3 expressly covers personal data for this age band.
