Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Clutch AI ("Clutch," "we," "our," or the "App") is operated by Conor Smith. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the App, why it is used, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

The short version: Clutch does not sell personal information. Your saved chat history is stored in encrypted, account-scoped storage on your device. Content you submit for an AI feature is sent through our backend to the provider needed to answer that request only after you affirmatively allow AI processing. You can revoke that permission or delete your account from the App.

2. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate your account and keep account-scoped data separated.
  • Provide Wingman, screenshot analysis, Profile Audit, Bio Writer, Date Spotter, Date Debrief, and Panic features.
  • Verify subscription access, process referrals, enforce usage limits, and prevent repeated trial abuse.
  • Send notifications you have enabled. Notification permission can be changed in your device settings.
  • Monitor reliability, investigate errors and abuse, and improve product flows.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations and protect users, the App, and our service providers.

We do not sell or rent personal information, use third-party advertising SDKs, or share your conversations with other Clutch users.

3. AI Requests and Screenshots

When you request an AI feature, Clutch sends the content needed for that request from your device to a Supabase Edge Function. The function may route text or image content to DeepSeek, OpenAI, or Anthropic, depending on the feature and service configuration. Date Spotter may also send a location-biased search to Google Places.

  • Before the first AI request, the App identifies the categories of content that may be shared, names the third-party AI providers, and asks you to allow that processing. You may decline and continue using non-AI account controls. You can revoke permission from Account > Privacy controls; no new AI request will be sent unless you allow processing again.
  • Only submit screenshots or messages that you have the right to use. Consider redacting names and other details that are not needed for the request.
  • Clutch compresses selected images for transmission, and temporary transmission copies made by the App are removed after processing. In Wingman, a device-local image reference may remain with the saved chat so you can revisit that conversation. In Date Debrief, the screenshot itself is not added to debrief history; only the transcript you review and confirm is saved. The original remains in your photo library unless you delete it there.
  • AI providers receive request content to produce an answer. Their handling and retention of API inputs is governed by our provider arrangements and their applicable policies; do not treat an AI request as a confidential or privileged communication.

5. Storage and Security

  • Saved conversations and related private state are account-scoped and encrypted on your device. The encryption key is stored using the device's secure credential storage.
  • Authentication session material is kept in secure device storage. Operational account, subscription, usage, referral, and notification records are stored with Supabase.
  • Network requests use encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Database access controls and server-side authorization are used to limit access to account data.
  • No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and store accounts secure, and contact us if you believe your Clutch account has been compromised.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • On-device private content: Saved conversations, bio versions, plans, and debriefs remain on the signed-in device until you delete the relevant record, sign out, delete your account, or remove the App or its data. Clutch limits each saved conversation to its most recent 1,000 messages.
  • AI request content: Is processed for the request rather than added to a cloud chat archive by Clutch. Service providers may retain request or security records under their own applicable terms and retention practices.
  • Account records: Are generally kept while your account is active. In-App account deletion removes your Supabase authentication account and linked profile records, then removes that account's private local App data from the device.
  • Trial-abuse ledger: Keyed hashes of the normalized email and device signal, together with the trial window, may remain after account deletion. They are scheduled for deletion after they are more than 18 months past the recorded trial window.
  • Purchases, analytics, diagnostics, and security records: May be retained by Clutch, app stores, or service providers for their operational, fraud-prevention, accounting, or legal needs, subject to their policies and applicable law.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can review or revoke third-party AI processing permission from Account > Privacy controls. AI features remain unavailable while permission is off, and non-AI account and privacy controls remain available.
  • Location, photo-library, and notification access can be declined or changed in device settings. Some related features will not work without the relevant access.
  • You can delete individual conversations, saved bio versions, and date debriefs in the App. Cancelling an active date plan removes that plan and its reminders.
  • You can delete your Clutch account from the account controls in the App. Cancel an active subscription separately through Apple App Store or Google Play so it does not renew.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of personal information. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity, and some records may be retained where permitted or required by law.

8. Age Requirement

Clutch is intended for users 16 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. Contact us if you believe an underage user has provided information to Clutch.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the App or applicable requirements change. We will update the date above and, when appropriate, provide additional notice in the App or through another reasonable channel.
